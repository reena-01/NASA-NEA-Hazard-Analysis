SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS
======================================================================
The dataset contains only a small sample of NASA Near-Earth Objects.
The dataset is also imbalanced between hazardous and non-hazardous classes.
Model results should therefore be interpreted as exploratory rather than production-ready.
The target represents the potentially hazardous classification used in the dataset.
The model identifies statistical relationships with this classification and does not establish physical causation or provide an official asteroid hazard assessment.
